🔀Lipolysis Sequence

TAG→ ATGL →DAG→ HSL →MAG→ MGL →Glycerol + 3 fatty acids
Regulation and compartment traps
  • TAG synthesis: mainly ER-associated, from glycerol-3-phosphate and activated fatty acids.
  • Adipose lipolysis occurs at lipid droplets, where ATGL starts breakdown of stored TAG.
  • DΦInsulin suppresses lipolysis and promotes TAG storage.
  • DΦEpinephrine: β-adrenergic → cAMP ↑ → PKA → HSL and perilipin phosphorylation → lipid mobilization ↑.
  • Glucagon supports fasting hepatic adaptation but is not the principal acute stimulator of human adipose lipolysis.
  • Cortisol modifies lipid metabolism over longer periods through transcriptional, tissue-specific actions.
  • Energy: TAG hydrolysis makes no ATP directly; oxidation of the released fatty acids does.
  • Board distractor: ATGL initiates TAG degradation; HSL acts mainly on DAG.

🌡️Fluidity, Synthesis and Traps

  • DLonger saturated acyl chains → membrane transition temperature ↑ (less fluid).
  • DMore cis-unsaturated fatty acids → kinks → membrane fluidity ↑.
  • ΦCholesterol buffers fluidity: limits disorder at high temperature and tight packing at low temperature.
  • ER = major site of glycerophospholipid synthesis; mitochondria = cardiolipin synthesis and remodeling.
  • ATP is used for specific transport and precursor-activation steps; membrane assembly has no ATP-producing yield.
  • PPS externalization signals apoptosis ("eat me") and provides a procoagulant surface.
  • High-yield trap: cardiolipin is characteristic of the inner mitochondrial membrane; defective remodeling → Barth syndrome.

  • DΦHMG-CoA reductase = rate-limiting enzyme of cholesterol synthesis; on the cytosol-facing ER membrane.
  • Cofactor: NADPH — 2 NADPH per mevalonate formed.
  • ΦInsulin generally favors hepatic cholesterol synthesis; glucagon/AMPK oppose reductase activity via phosphorylation.
  • CSterol excess → SREBP-dependent transcription ↓ and reductase degradation ↑.
  • Statins are competitive HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors → hepatic LDL receptor ↑ SUPPLEMENTARY.
  • Net ATP: cholesterol synthesis consumes ATP and reducing equivalents; it produces none.
  • Compartment trap: cholesterol synthesis = cytosol/ER (cytosolic HMG-CoA synthase); mitochondrial HMG-CoA synthase = ketogenesis.
  • Cholesterol vs cholesteryl ester: amphipathic membrane sterol vs hydrophobic storage/transport form.

Regulation and drug targets
  • PLA₂ release of arachidonic acid often controls precursor availability.
  • COX-1 = constitutive; COX-2 = induced by inflammatory signaling.
  • DGlucocorticoids suppress PLA₂-associated signaling and inflammatory enzyme expression → prostanoids and leukotrienes ↓.
  • DNSAIDs inhibit COX; aspirin irreversibly acetylates COX → platelet TXA₂ ↓ for the platelet’s lifespan (platelets cannot make new enzyme) SUPPLEMENTARY.
  • PAspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease: COX block shunts arachidonate toward 5-LOX → leukotrienes ↑ → bronchospasm SUPPLEMENTARY.
  • 5-LOX makes leukotriene intermediates, not prostaglandins. Energy: no ATP-producing yield.

🧭High-Yield Distinctions

Gq receptor→PLC→PIP₂→DAG (membrane) → PKC+IP₃ (cytosol) → ER Ca²⁺
  • DΦDAG: membrane-bound activator of selected PKC isoforms.
  • DΦIP₃: soluble messenger that triggers ER calcium release.
  • DΦPIP₃: membrane recruitment signal for AKT pathway components.
  • PPTEN opposes PI3K; loss of PTEN → unchecked AKT growth signaling (tumor suppressor) SUPPLEMENTARY.
  • S1P regulates immune-cell trafficking (lymphocyte egress) and vascular responses.
  • ΦInsulin activates PI3K–AKT metabolic signaling.
  • Energy: signaling may consume ATP for phosphorylation but is not ATP-generating. Compartments: plasma-membrane phosphoinositides, cytosolic proteins, ER Ca²⁺ stores.

⚠️Testing Pitfalls

  • Fabry: enzyme activity is informative in affected males; females need molecular testing because activity may be normal.
  • Krabbe: low GALC alone may reflect pseudodeficiency; psychosine and molecular findings add specificity.
  • MLD: ARSA pseudodeficiency means an isolated low enzyme value needs confirmation.
  • Tay–Sachs = hexosaminidase A deficiency, not sphingomyelinase deficiency.
  • Niemann–Pick C = defective NPC1/NPC2 intracellular cholesterol trafficking — distinct from SMPD1-related Niemann–Pick A/B.
  • DPRefsum: α-oxidation block → phytanic acid ↑ (dietary, from dairy/ruminant fat) → retinitis pigmentosa, neuropathy, ichthyosis SUPPLEMENTARY.
  • DPX-ALD: ABCD1 transporter defect → VLCFAs cannot enter peroxisomes → VLCFA ↑ → adrenal and white-matter injury.

Final Sprint

TOS priority closing sequence

⚖️Classic Board Distractor Pairs — All Plates

⚡ Fatty Acids
  • ACC makes malonyl-CoA (synthesis); CPT I is inhibited by malonyl-CoA (oxidation entry).
  • ACC = fatty acid synthesis; HMG-CoA reductase = cholesterol synthesis.
  • Mitochondrial β-oxidation = long-chain FAs, FADH₂/NADH; peroxisomal β-oxidation = VLCFAs, H₂O₂.
  • β-oxidation = straight chains; α-oxidation = branched phytanic acid (Refsum).
🧈 Triacylglycerol
  • ATGL initiates TAG hydrolysis; HSL acts mainly on DAG.
  • LPL clears circulating TAG (ApoC-II activates); HSL mobilizes stored adipose TAG.
  • Insulin stores TAG and blocks lipolysis; epinephrine drives cAMP–PKA lipolysis.
🫧 Membrane Phospholipids
  • PLA₂ releases arachidonic acid; PLC generates DAG + IP₃.
  • Phosphatidylcholine = glycerol-based; sphingomyelin = ceramide-based phospholipid.
  • Cardiolipin = inner mitochondrial membrane; sphingomyelin = myelin/plasma membranes.
  • PS inside = healthy cell; PS outside = apoptosis/procoagulant surface.
🧠 Sphingolipids
  • Gaucher = glucosylceramide; Niemann–Pick = sphingomyelin.
  • Tay–Sachs = GM2 ganglioside; Krabbe = psychosine.
  • Fabry = X-linked; other sphingolipidoses = autosomal recessive.
  • Ceramide and sphingosine-1-phosphate are distinct sphingolipid signals (S1P = lymphocyte egress).
🥚 Cholesterol
  • HMG-CoA reductase = cholesterol synthesis (cytosol/ER); mitochondrial HMG-CoA synthase = ketogenesis.
  • Cholesterol = amphipathic membrane sterol; cholesteryl ester = hydrophobic storage form.
  • LCAT esterifies in plasma (ApoA-I); ACAT esterifies inside cells.
🔥 Eicosanoids
  • COX → prostanoids; LOX → leukotrienes.
  • TXA₂ = platelet aggregation, vasoconstriction; PGI₂ = antiplatelet, vasodilation.
  • Aspirin = irreversible COX acetylation; other NSAIDs = reversible COX inhibition.
  • Glucocorticoids reduce prostanoids and leukotrienes; NSAIDs reduce prostanoids only.
📡 Second Messengers
  • DAG = membrane, activates PKC; IP₃ = soluble, releases ER Ca²⁺.
  • PI3K makes PIP₃; PTEN removes it.
⚗️ Reactions & Properties
  • Iodine value = unsaturation; saponification value = average chain length (shorter → higher).
  • Hydrogenation raises saturation; peroxidation damages PUFAs.
  • Peroxidation = radical damage; β-oxidation = controlled catabolism.
🩺 Deficiency & Metabolite Patterns
  • Refsum = phytanic acid α-oxidation; X-ALD = VLCFA transport/oxidation.
  • Niemann–Pick A/B = SMPD1; Niemann–Pick C = NPC1/2 cholesterol trafficking.
  • Smith–Lemli–Opitz = 7-DHC ↑; CTX = cholestanol ↑.

🏁Final PLE Mental Checklist — All Plates

⚡ Fatty Acids
  • Palmitate synthesis: ACC; biotin; 7 ATP + 14 NADPH consumed.
  • Palmitate oxidation: CPT I-mediated import; ≈106 ATP net after complete oxidation.
🧈 Triacylglycerol
  • TAG lipolysis: ATGL → HSL → MGL.
🫧 Membrane Phospholipids
  • Membrane fluidity: cis-unsaturation increases fluidity; cholesterol buffers it.
🧠 Sphingolipids
  • Sphingolipid synthesis: serine palmitoyltransferase; PLP (vitamin B6).
  • Gaucher: glucosylceramide. Tay–Sachs: GM2. Niemann–Pick A/B: sphingomyelin. Fabry: Gb3. Krabbe: psychosine.
🥚 Cholesterol
  • Cholesterol synthesis: HMG-CoA reductase; inhibited by statins.
🔥 Eicosanoids
  • Eicosanoids: PLA₂ releases arachidonic acid; COX makes prostanoids; LOX makes leukotrienes.
📡 Second Messengers
  • PIP₂ → DAG + IP₃ via PLC; PIP₂ → PIP₃ via PI3K.
⚗️ Reactions & Properties
  • Vitamin E protects PUFAs; GPX4 (selenium) reduces lipid hydroperoxides.
🩺 Deficiency Diseases
  • Refsum: phytanic acid. Smith–Lemli–Opitz: 7-dehydrocholesterol. X-ALD: VLCFA.
🔎 Metabolite → Diagnosis
  • Most useful sequence: enzyme defect → accumulated lipid → affected tissue → characteristic phenotype → confirmatory biochemical or molecular test.
